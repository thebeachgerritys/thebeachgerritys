Story

How we met

Ours is a modern-day love story: we met online. Our first date was on a rooftop patio that we had all to ourselves.

Honestly, it felt a bit like a client meeting. Jackie talked about the job market so much that Kevin was sure she wasn't interested. She was.

Since then we've explored many more rooftops, survived two hurricanes, traveled to the Netherlands (twice), England, Sweden, Denmark, France and Mexico, camped in Kevin's front yard during a flood rehab, and embraced beach life by catching as many sunsets as possible.

The proposal

On June 12th, we got in the car to meet Jackie's parents and Kaylie for dinner. Kevin drove the other way, to the marina near our house, where the Fantasea, a 46-foot sailboat, was waiting as our private charter. We sailed out to the sunset off Pass-a-Grille Beach, Kevin got down on one knee, and Jackie said yes.

New Haven

The Blake sits in the middle of downtown, steps from Yale's campus. If you're in town for the weekend, here's how we'd spend a winter afternoon.

Eat · Wooster Street

New Haven Apizza

The city's coal-fired, thin-crust pizza is the reason people drive here. Frank Pepe's (get the white clam pie), Sally's Apizza and Modern Apizza are the classics. Expect a line.

Art · Steps from The Blake

Yale's Art Museums

The Yale University Art Gallery and the Yale Center for British Art face each other on Chapel Street. Both are world-class and both are free.

Museum · About a mile north

Yale Peabody Museum

Dinosaurs, gems, and one of the great natural history collections in the country, newly renovated and free to visit.

Walk · Yale campus

Beinecke Library

A short walk through campus brings you to the Beinecke, where translucent marble walls glow around a tower of rare books, including a Gutenberg Bible. Free.

Sweets · Little Italy

Wooster Street Pastries

While you're on Wooster Street for pizza, stop for cannoli and Italian ices. It's New Haven's Little Italy, and dessert is part of the tradition.

Evening · Downtown

A Show or a Nightcap

The historic Shubert Theatre and College Street Music Hall are a short walk from the hotel, with plenty of cocktail bars in between. Check what's playing that weekend.

Hours change seasonally, so check before you go.
